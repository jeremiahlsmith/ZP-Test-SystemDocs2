---
title: "Eligibility verification"
description: "Check patient insurance coverage before a visit to reduce claim denials."
---

Eligibility verification confirms that a patient's insurance is active and shows their benefits before services are rendered. Most denials caused by inactive coverage can be prevented with this check.

## Run a single check

1. Open the patient record.
2. Select **Insurance** > **Verify eligibility**.
3. Choose the service type, such as **Office visit** or **Anesthesia**.
4. Select **Run check**.

Results usually return in under 10 seconds.

## Batch verification

Insurance Billing automatically verifies every patient on the next day's schedule at 2:00 AM local time. Review the results each morning under **Schedule** > **Eligibility queue**.

| Status | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active | Coverage confirmed | None |
| Inactive | Coverage terminated or not found | Contact the patient for updated insurance |
| Needs review | Payer returned partial data | Call the payer or rerun the check |
| Error | Payer system unavailable | Retry later |

## What the response includes

- Plan name and group number
- Copay, coinsurance, and remaining deductible
- Out-of-pocket maximum
- Prior authorization requirements
- Coordination of benefits details

<Tip>
  Rerun eligibility for any patient whose last check is older than 30 days.
</Tip>
