Insurance Billing helps practices submit cleaner claims, get paid faster, and spend less time chasing denials. It connects your practice management system to payers through a single clearinghouse integration.

<Note>
  This is sample documentation for demonstration purposes. Product names, features, and values are fictional.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify eligibility">
    Check patient coverage in real time before the visit. See [Eligibility verification](/insurance-billing/eligibility-verification).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit claims">
    Scrub and send claims electronically in the 837P or 837I format. See [Claim submission](/insurance-billing/claim-submission).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve denials">
    Triage rejected and denied claims with suggested fixes. See [Denial management](/insurance-billing/denial-management).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track performance">
    Monitor collections, days in A/R, and first-pass yield. See [Reporting](/insurance-billing/reporting).
  </Step>
</Steps>
